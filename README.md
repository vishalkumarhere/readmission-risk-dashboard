# Patient Readmission Risk & Care Coordination Intelligence Platform

A predictive analytics pipeline and decision dashboard that scores 30 day readmission
risk at discharge, segments patients into risk tiers, and models the care coordination
follow up capacity needed to reduce preventable readmissions and HRRP penalty exposure.

Built as a healthcare analytics portfolio project. All data on the live demo is
illustrative synthetic data modeled on publicly documented CMS readmission risk
drivers. No real patient data or PHI is used.

Live demo: https://readmission-risk-dashboard.vercel.app

Stack: Python, XGBoost, pandas, Flask concepts, inline SVG/HTML dashboard.
