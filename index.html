Patient Readmission Risk & Care Coordination
Intelligence Platform

A predictive analytics pipeline that scores 30 day readmission risk at discharge, segments patients into actionable risk tiers, and models the follow up capacity a care coordination team needs to hit a target reduction in preventable readmissions and HRRP penalty exposure.

All patient, admission, and outcome figures on this page are illustrative synthetic data modeled on publicly documented CMS readmission risk drivers (prior admissions, length of stay, comorbidity burden, discharge disposition, follow up scheduling). No real patient data, PHI, or Henry Ford Health data is used anywhere on this page.

Care coordination action plan
  • 01Call high risk discharges within 48 hours. 441 patients score in the high risk tier; the model ranks them by score x days remaining in the 30 day window so coordinators work the most time sensitive cases first.
  • 02Force a scheduled follow up visit before discharge for anyone missing one. Missing a scheduled follow up is the single largest driver of readmission risk in this cohort, ahead of comorbidity count.
  • 03Flag SNF and rehab discharges for a 72 hour check in call. Discharge disposition to a facility rather than home carries roughly double the readmission odds of a home discharge in this data.
  • 04Route medium risk patients to a lighter touch nurse line follow up. Reserves coordinator time for the high risk tier while still covering the medium tier, which is nearly a third of all discharges.
